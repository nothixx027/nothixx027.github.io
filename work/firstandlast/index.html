first & last

A crazy drinking party game for adults — 90 cards of pure chaos. Think fast or drink fast.

First & Last is a fast-paced party game built around one rule: react first or drink last. Designed in-house at Dring as part of their card-game lineup, the project covered the full identity from card faces to retail packaging — a competitive system you can explain in fifteen seconds and play for hours.

Card faces and back design tuned for a 30-second decision.

The visual language leans into competition without taking itself seriously. A bold typographic mark on the box, high-contrast colour blocks on the cards, and an unmistakable hierarchy so players can read prompts at speed in low-light rooms. Built to look right in a hand, on a table, and on someone's Instagram story.
