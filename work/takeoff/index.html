takeoff

The ultimate darts party game — hang it on a wall, throw the magnetic darts, drink or make someone else drink.

Takeoff is Dring's dartboard party game — a compact black-and-yellow board split into "sip" and "make someone else drink" zones, plus four magnetic darts safe enough for a moving crowd. Everything's designed for a small apartment: forgiving throw, quick rules, and a hook on the back so it goes on the wall in 15 seconds.

One dartboard, four magnetic darts — everything needed to start a game.

The visual system leans into the split — purple for "you drink," yellow for "make someone else drink." Every design choice, from the flipped upside-down type to the hand-drawn arrows, was tuned to be legible in low light, from a couch, with your hands on someone else's shoulder.

02

Tested at real gatherings. Loud, competitive, and forgiving of missed shots.

Takeoff is now a staple in Dring's lineup. The board hangs quiet on the wall until someone walks in, spots it, and asks how it works.
